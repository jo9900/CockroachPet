## Description (English)

A realistic cockroach that lives on your macOS desktop. It roams freely, reacts to your cursor, and is nearly impossible to get rid of — just like the real thing.

**Features:**
• Realistic AI behavior — patrol, idle, wall-following, and sudden dashes
• Reacts to your cursor — flee when you move fast, approach when you stay still
• Click to flip it on its back and watch it struggle
• Double-click for a surprise — it might burst into baby cockroaches!
• Drag it around — legs flailing in the air
• Corner it near a screen edge and watch it fly away
• Baby cockroaches that grow up over time
• Customize max population (up to 99!) and growth speed
• Persistent — your cockroaches survive app restarts
• Menu bar app — no Dock icon clutter

**Warning:** May cause sudden urge to check under your desk. 🪳
