Privacy Policy

CockroachPet — Last updated: March 2026

Overview

CockroachPet does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your local device.

Data We Collect

None. CockroachPet does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or crash reports.

Local Storage

The app stores your preferences (such as settings) locally on your Mac using macOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

CockroachPet does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics services, or advertising networks.

Network Access

CockroachPet does not make any network requests.
